Organizational boundary statement · FY2025

This statement sets out the organizational boundary used for Cascade Provisions Co.'s greenhouse gas inventory for the year ended 31 December 2025, prepared under the GHG Protocol Corporate Standard.

The company consolidates on an operational-control basis for the 2025 reporting year.

The boundary includes the Tualatin Plant, the Kent Cannery and Modesto Bottling, each wholly owned and operated by the company. Leased office space in Portland is excluded as immaterial (below 0.5% of estimated emissions).
